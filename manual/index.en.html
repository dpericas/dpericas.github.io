    👋 Getting started

    The club app has the schedule and results of every team, live matches, club notices, squad call-ups and much more. Tap a section to open it.

    Installing the app (Android)

    The app is not on Google Play: you download it from the club website, dpericas.github.io/app, or by scanning the QR code on the poster.

    1. Tap “Download for Android”.
    2. Open the downloaded file. If your phone asks, allow “Install apps from this source”.
    3. Open the app: Pili welcomes you.

    💡 Updates arrive on their own: when there is a new one, the app tells you and installs it with one tap.

    I have an iPhone

    Use the website dpericas.github.io: it has the home page, the schedule and the club. In Safari, tap Share › “Add to Home Screen” and it will work like an app.

    The welcome: teams, notifications and “Who are you?”

    The first time, the app asks you:

    • Which teams you follow (you mark them with ★): they come first, you get their reminders and you see them together in “My matches”.
    • Which notifications you want: club notices, match reminders and live matches.
    • Who you are: player, family, coach, board member or just a fan. If you choose player or family, “My team” opens at the end so you can ask to join your team. If you choose coach or board, the Editors' area opens so you can create your account and ask for access (an administrator approves it).

    You can see it again in Club › ⚙️ › About the app › Welcome.

    The four tabs
    TabWhat you find there
    🏐 TeamsAll the club's teams, the countdown to the next match, today's matches.
    📅 ScheduleAll matches by day, squad call-ups, car sharing, directions.
    ℹ️ ClubNotices, training times, gallery, halls, Pili, “My team” and Preferences.
    🔴 ScoreboardKeeping the score of a match, broadcasting it and following live matches.

    👉 You can swipe left and right to move from one tab to another.

    🏐 Teams

    Team page: matches, standings and squad

    Tap a team to see its matches and standings (from the federation's official website) and the squad with photos. Non-federated teams (School, Masters…) show the squad, training times and friendlies.

    Tap a team's star ★ to follow it. Teams on a winning streak get a 🔥.

    Countdown and today's matches

    At the very top there is the countdown to the next match of your ★ teams and, on match days, the list of today's matches.

    Keeping score from the team page

    Open a match on the team page: if it hasn't been played yet, a small 🔴 button appears so you can keep its score with the teams already filled in.

    📅 Schedule

    Matches by day and “My matches”

    The schedule has all the club's matches (official ones and friendlies) sorted by day. With the 👪 My matches button you see only your teams' matches, with a colour per team, a warning if two matches clash and who is driving.

    Squad call-ups and “coming / not coming”

    When the coach publishes a call-up, the match shows the 👥 button with the number of players called up. There you see the meeting point, the venue, the kit and who is called up.

    If you are a team member and have said who you are in the squad (see My team), you'll get the “You are called up!” notification and, inside the call-up, you can answer Coming or Not coming (with a reason). The coach sees the summary.

    Directions and sports hall guide

    Tap Directions on a match to open Google Maps to the hall. Away matches get a mark when the hall tips are saved so you can check them without coverage.

    In Club › Halls there is a map of the halls and tips from families (parking, café, access…). You can add tips too.

    Car sharing

    For away matches, tap Car sharing: you can offer seats in your car, join a car or say you need a seat. Entries delete themselves two days after the match.

    Phone calendar and printable schedule

    In Preferences you can have your ★ teams' matches added to your phone's calendar. From a team page you can get the season schedule as a PDF to print.

    ℹ️ Club

    Notices (including urgent and 🔒 team ones)

    Club notices appear at the top of the Club tab. Urgent ones come first and in red, with their own sound. Some include an image or a PDF.

    Notices with 🔒 are team only: only the team's approved players and families, the coaches and the board can see them.

    My team (players and families)
    1. Club › 👤 My team.
    2. Choose Player (one team only) or Family (one or more teams) and type the name.
    3. Choose the teams and, for each team, who you are in the squad (or your children).
    4. Send request. The team's coach or the board approves it.

    Once it shows “✅ In”, you get the team's 🔒 notices and your personal call-up notification, and you can answer “coming / not coming”. You can change the name or the children without losing the approval.

    💡 If it shows “❔ Not approved”, tap “Send request” again.

    Training times, gallery, news, values and warm-up
    • Training: each team's training sessions.
    • Gallery: the club's photo albums; families can suggest photos, which the coach reviews.
    • News: what the federation publishes about the club.
    • Values: the club's code.
    • Warm-up: guided routines with a timer.
    • Volleyball guide: the rules explained, with a quiz.
    Kit orders

    When the board opens an order, the 👕 tile appears in Club: you choose items, sizes and quantities and send your request.

    🔴 Scoreboard and live matches

    Keeping the score of a match
    1. Scoreboard tab. In Today's matches tap “🏐 Keep score” on a match (the teams and rules are filled in), or type the teams yourself.
    2. Choose the rules (best of 5, of 3, under-12s…) and who serves first.
    3. Tap a team's panel to give it a point. Undo fixes the last one.

    There are timeouts with a countdown, substitutions, the court switch in the tie-break, set point and match point alerts, and a “drama mode” when the match is very close.

    Line-up, rotations and substitutions

    With “👥 Pick from the squad” you tap the 6 players in position order (I is the server, II, III, IV, V, VI). The scoreboard shows the court with the positions, who is serving, who serves next and the rotation (R1–R6). When the team wins back the serve you see “🔄 rotate · X serves”.

    In Substitutions you choose who goes off and who comes on: the new player takes the same position.

    Broadcasting live

    With the scoreboard open, tap Broadcast: families can follow the match point by point with a link or a code and, if the match is on the club's list, in “Follow a live match”. Followers can clap 👏, play the drum 🥁 and send reactions, and you see them on the scoreboard.

    Follow a live match

    In the Scoreboard tab, Follow a live match shows the club's live matches; you can also open it from a link received on WhatsApp. With live match alerts turned on, the app tells you when a match starts and at the end of each set.

    Big screen, history, match report and result image

    The big screen shows the score in large type for the hall (ideal on a tablet or TV). The history holds your saved matches: you can make a PDF match report and a result image to share.

    🐣 Pili

    Your Pili, stickers, wallpapers and photo booth

    Pili is the club mascot. In Your Pili you choose what she looks like (green and black, stripes, champion, spiker… or the old yellow ones) and the costumes you unlock by playing. There are also WhatsApp stickers, wallpapers and a photo booth with a club frame.

    If you tap her, she jumps, spins, winks or talks (with sounds, which you can turn off in Preferences).

    Games and leaderboard

    Keepy-uppy, reception, block, memory and the rules quiz, with a club leaderboard by nickname. There are hidden secrets…

    Pili's path: 8 stages, from School to Masters (Keepy-uppy, Reception, Serve, Setting, Block, Spike, Rotations and Beach vs Pili). Every game has 5 ⭐: 2 ⭐ in a stage open the next one; 3 ⭐ unlock the night variant, 5 ⭐ the turbo, and 30 ⭐ in total the mirror. Every new star gives a sticker.

    Daily challenge: one game a day, the same for everyone, with today's ranking and a streak of days in a row. More games: quiz, memory, referee badge (4 levels, a badge to share) and the seasonal game, open only on festive days, with special stickers.

    My collection: medals (some secret), sticker album, the Pili pet (feed her and play so she levels up) and the team league: pick your team and your ⭐, medals and stickers add points for it (you need a ranking nickname).

    💾 Save progress: create a code (XXXXX-XXXXX) and get your games back on another phone or on the club website (dpericas.github.io/jocs, iPhone too). With the code it saves by itself after every game and merges the progress of all your devices; anyone with the code can use your progress.

    ⚙️ Preferences

    Appearance, intro, language and text size

    Club › ⚙️: light or dark theme, appearance (Club, Night in pure black, Court, Retro), the intro when opening the app (club video, with theme sound effects that can be turned off, animated Pili or none), the language (Catalan, Spanish, English) and the text size.

    Notifications and the sound of each one

    Turn club notices, match reminders and live match alerts on or off. In Sound of each notification you choose the sound for notices, urgent notices and reminders (whistle, drum, bell, Pili's voice, alarm, phone sound or silent).

    Backup, privacy and updates
    • Backup: to switch phones, export and import the history, your ★ teams and preferences.
    • Anonymous usage statistics: they help the board know how many people use the app; they also send error reports if the app closes by itself. You can turn them off.
    • About the app: the version, “Check for updates”, “What's new” and “Welcome”.

    📱 Home screen

    Widgets and shortcuts
    • Next match: your teams' upcoming matches.
    • Pili: cheers on your teams depending on the results.
    • Live: the score of the club match being played now, point by point (turn on live match alerts).

    If you long-press the app icon there are shortcuts to Agenda, Live and My team.

    🧑‍🏫 Coaches coaches · board · admin

    Signing in to the Editors' area
    1. At the bottom of the Club tab, tap “✏️ Coach or board member? Sign in here”.
    2. Sign in with the e-mail and password of the account the club created for you and request access.
    3. Once the board approves you, the Coaches and board section appears in Club.

    You can also do it from a computer at dpericas.github.io/editors (see Website).

    Posting notices, activities, friendlies, training times and photos

    In the Editors' area you choose what you want to post. A coach only posts for their own teams; the board, for the whole club. Notices can be urgent and include an image or a PDF.

    Turn on 🔒 Team only so only team members can see it (no image or PDF).

    ⚡ Quick message to the team

    Templates in two taps: training cancelled, change of time or place, kit, “remember to bring”, match postponed, meeting point or free text. By default it is team only and expires by itself.

    Squad call-ups and answers

    From the schedule, tap 👥 on one of your team's matches: meeting point, venue, kit, players called up and notes. It is published in the app and can be sent on WhatsApp. Inside the call-up you see Coming / Not coming / No answer with the reasons. There are also private notes for the match, for coaches only.

    Players and families, squads and suggested photos
    • 👥 Players and families: each team shows its pending requests (“⏳ 2”); approve, reject or remove. “⚠️ duplicate” flags repeated names.
    • 📋 Squads (non-federated teams): names, role and shirt number. They are used on the team page, in call-ups, the wheel and “My team”.
    • Suggested photos: review the photos families suggest before publishing them.
    Coach's wheel

    Choose your team, remove whoever isn't there and spin the wheel (who starts, who picks up the balls…).

    🏛️ Board board · admin

    Weekly “Upcoming matches” message and WhatsApp

    In the Editors' area, Upcoming matches prepares the week's message for the club WhatsApp (matches, halls with a link and teams with a bye). Every post has a “Post to WhatsApp” button with the image and the text.

    Editors, requests and app usage

    In Editors and usage you approve access requests, choose the role (coach or board) and the teams, and see the anonymous app usage. It also notifies you when someone requests access.

    Kit orders

    Open and close orders with the items and sizes, and download the summary of requests.

    🛠️ Administration admin

    The administrator can do everything the board does and also manages the administrators and can delete anything. The section is in Club › 🛠️ Administration.

    Simulate a profile

    See the app the way a general user, a team's coach, the board, a player or a family sees it (with the teams and names you choose). A purple banner appears; the ✕ stops it. Anything you post is still posted with your own account.

    Monitoring: activity log and errors

    Log: who posted, edited or deleted what and when. Errors: unexpected app crashes on phones, grouped together.

    Remote switches, test notification, data and diagnostics
    • Switches: turn off features (car sharing, tips, photos, leaderboard, supporters), show a message at the top of the app or ask people to update old versions, without publishing a new version.
    • Real test notification: only to your phone or to the editors.
    • Data: how many documents each collection has, and delete them.
    • Diagnostics: version, Android, phone and identifiers (can be copied).

    🌐 Club website

    The website for everyone

    dpericas.github.io: home page with the notices, the teams' schedule, a printable schedule and the club. You can install it on your phone (especially on iPhone).

    Following a live match on the website: the link of a broadcast match (dpericas.github.io/live/#vsp-…) shows the score just like in the app (serve, rotations, streaks, timeouts, set card) and how many people are watching 👀. From there you can cheer too: 👏 clap, 🥁 play the drum and send reactions, which reach whoever is keeping score.

    Editors' area on the website

    dpericas.github.io/editors (link at the bottom of the website): sign in with the same account as in the app. You can post notices (🔒 ones too), approve players and families and, if you are an admin, manage editors, requests, switches and the log.

    💡 Only tick “Remember me on this computer” on your own computer.

    ❓ Questions and problems

    I'm not getting notifications
    • Check that notifications are turned on in Club › ⚙️.
    • On your phone: Settings › Apps › Vòlei Santpedor › Notifications on, and no battery saving for the app.
    • Live match notifications need “Live match alerts” turned on (it leaves a small permanent notification).
    My team request isn't approved

    Open Club › My team and refresh. If it shows “Not approved”, tap “Send request” again and tell the coach.

    The schedule isn't up to date

    The schedule is read from the federation's official website when you open the app. Open it with a connection and tap refresh.

    🔒 Privacy

    What data the app uses
    • The app doesn't ask for your e-mail (only editors have an account).
    • Each phone has an anonymous identifier. The name you enter in “My team” is only seen by your teams' coaches and the board.
    • Statistics and error reports are anonymous and can be turned off.
    • “🔒 team” notices can only be read by the team's approved members.
